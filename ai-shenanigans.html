P.T. Barnum's Creepy Cryptids

P.T. Barnum's Creepy Cryptids is a vintage horror art-book concept that reimagines obscure folkloric creatures as the headline attractions of a lost nineteenth-century traveling sideshow. Each creature is treated as both scholarship and spectacle: one page reads like an antique encyclopedia entry full of lore, maps, warnings, and taxonomy, while the facing page explodes into a sensational Barnum-style broadside. The result is a cabinet of international nightmares where eerie research meets carnival showmanship, and every spread asks what dreadful thing Barnum has dragged back this time.
