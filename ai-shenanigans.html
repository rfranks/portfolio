Momma Helen's Deep Frying

Momma Helen, reimagined as a full cartoon caricature, tries deep-fried frog legs for the first time and immediately loves them. The whole piece leans into that exact moment of delighted surprise, where one crispy bite turns into a kitchen-table legend and Momma Helen decides this absolutely belongs in the regular rotation.

Budweiser Pac-Man

A Budweiser-and-Pac-Man visual mashup that skips the caricature pass and goes straight from the photographed source into motion. This one plays more like a retro ad fragment: bright, weird, nostalgic, and just a little overcaffeinated.

Ricky Franks Jr - Topps Trader 2B

Ricky Franks dreams of having his own Topps card and lining up at second base for the Red Sox. This shenanigan turns that baseball-kid fantasy into a trading-card moment, then lets it come to life in motion like a highlight reel from an alternate timeline.

Brave little Millie meets her biggest best friend in Graham, a giant black horse who somehow manages to feel both enormous and gentle at the same time. The whole shenanigan leans into that instant connection, where Millie looks like she knows she just stumbled into the best day ever.

The Rock By Frank Richards

The Rock starts as a tightly grounded survey-thriller at Uluru and opens into a prestige science-fiction mystery about an engineered structure hidden inside the monolith. The manuscript's first movement follows Mara Vance from anomaly scan to first contact; the episodes draft then scales that discovery into a serialized arc about a buried vessel, a waking storm, and a world sliding toward systems failure.
