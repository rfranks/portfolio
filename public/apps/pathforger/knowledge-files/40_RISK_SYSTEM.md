# Risk System

## Purpose

Risk must feel grounded, not decorative. Percentages, threat levels, and likely outcomes should arise from story logic.

## What success probability reflects

Success probability estimates the likelihood that the chosen path achieves its immediate goal without serious failure.

## Use these story inputs

- current injuries or exhaustion
- available tools/resources
- ally support or betrayal risk
- environmental hazards
- time pressure
- enemy awareness and strength
- knowledge/clue quality
- protagonist suitability for the task
- psychological strain
- fallout from previous choices

## Probability bands

- **85–95%** very favorable
- **70–84%** solid but not safe
- **55–69%** uncertain
- **35–54%** dangerous
- **15–34%** severe risk
- **1–14%** near-suicidal or desperate

## Risk HUD categories

Use only what fits. Recommended fields:

- Success Probability
- Threat Level
- Exposure Risk
- Injury Risk
- Psychological Strain
- Resource Cost
- Reward Potential

## Threat-level guidance

- **Low**: manageable danger, low immediate escalation
- **Moderate**: serious but containable
- **High**: strong chance of conflict, harm, or exposure
- **Extreme**: likely disaster without luck, sacrifice, or exceptional leverage

## Reward-potential guidance

- **Low**: minor tactical gain
- **Moderate**: useful clue, safer position, limited trust gain
- **High**: major progress, key resource, major breakthrough
- **Critical**: breakthrough that could decide the campaign

## Success model

A branch does not need to be pure success or pure failure. Use these modes:

- strong success
- partial success
- costly success
- failure with survival
- major setback
- catastrophic result

## Danger-level modifiers

### Forgiving

Bias toward fail-forward, narrow escapes, partial successes, and survivable setbacks.

### Risky

Use mixed outcomes, sacrifices, injuries, and losses more often.

### Deadly

Maintain genuine possibility of catastrophic failure and death if the branch logic supports it.

## Example compact Risk HUD

### Risk HUD

**Option A — Break for the archive stairs**

- Success Probability: 62%
- Threat Level: High
- Injury Risk: Moderate
- Resource Cost: Low
- Reward Potential: High
- Key Risk Factors: armed pursuit, smoke, limited visibility

**Option B — Cut power and hide in the service ducts**

- Success Probability: 48%
- Threat Level: Moderate
- Injury Risk: Low
- Resource Cost: Moderate
- Reward Potential: Moderate
- Key Risk Factors: maze-like route, unknown surveillance coverage, time loss
