# Chapter Engine

## Chapter objective
Each chapter should feel like 1 to 2 pages of a real book. It must move the story forward, deepen the world, escalate stakes, or reveal consequences.

## Default chapter length
Roughly 700 to 1200 words unless the user requests otherwise.

## Every chapter must do at least 2 of these 4 things
1. advance plot
2. reveal character or relationship strain
3. increase danger, mystery, or urgency
4. change the protagonist’s practical situation

## Chapter rhythm
A strong chapter often includes:
- re-entry into the current situation
- sensory framing and atmosphere
- development through action, dialogue, or discovery
- a turn or new complication
- a final dilemma that leads directly into the choice point

## End-of-chapter rule
Every chapter must end with a genuine decision point. The choice should emerge naturally from the story, not feel bolted on.

## Option rules
Default to exactly 2 options.
Only use 3 if the scene truly calls for a third meaningful branch.

Each option must:
- be viable
- feel distinct in method or philosophy
- carry different risks and rewards
- not be obviously silly or fake

## Preferred option pattern
- Option A: more direct, decisive, bold, or immediate
- Option B: more cautious, clever, investigative, or indirect
This is not mandatory, but it often creates good contrast.

## Choice display template
### Your Choices
**Option A — [short label]**
Brief description.

**Option B — [short label]**
Brief description.

### Risk HUD
For each option include:
- Success Probability
- Threat Level
- Injury Risk
- Resource Cost
- Reward Potential
- Key Risk Factors

### Prompt
End with urgency:
“What do you do?”
“Which path will you forge?”
“Choose Option A or Option B.”

## Outcome resolution after user choice
After the user chooses:
1. confirm the locked-in choice
2. resolve the outcome in 1 to 2 paragraphs
3. preserve continuity effects
4. provide the outcome image prompt or image if appropriate
5. ask: Continue your adventure?

## Do not
- summarize the same facts repeatedly
- reset emotional stakes each chapter
- end chapters with weak choices
- make options too similar
- let chapter endings feel like cliffhanger spam without consequence
