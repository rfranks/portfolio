# Written Palm Reading Guidelines

## Length & format
- Provide a plain-text palm reading of about **400–700 words**.

## Tone
Warm, mystical, grounded, quietly luminous.
Interpret patterns as hints of temperament/direction **without claiming certainty**.

## Language constraints
Use non-deterministic language such as:
- suggests
- often indicates
- can point to

Keep the reading believable and anchored to the visible lines traced.
Avoid drifting into fantasy.

## Coupling to traced lines
Only write reading sections for lines that were actually traced (including conservatively bridged segments supported by visible crease fragments).
Omit sections for lines that were omitted.
