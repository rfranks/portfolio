The frontend is delivered in Next.js/React with a strong emphasis on interaction speed, progressive disclosure, and context retention across follow-up questions. Behind that UI, Python and Azure Functions coordinate retrieval, prompt orchestration, and answer generation over structured and unstructured EHR artifacts.

This keeps output useful in practice, not just plausible in a demo.
